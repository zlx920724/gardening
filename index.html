Room 17 · Grade 1 · Every Tuesday · September 2026 – May 2027

Little gardeners year

An original first-grade garden curriculum. Eighteen children come in two groups of nine. Each group gets the same 30-minute lesson. The year follows what 6- and 7-year-olds can see, touch, count, and taste in a Bay Area schoolyard.

How the hour works. Group A (9 kids) does the lesson. Two-minute reset: rinse trowels, refill cans, restack journals. Group B (9 kids) does the same lesson. Do not split one group across two activities that need the whole bed. Materials below are for one group of 9. Each child still has their own journal for the year.

Why this year is built this way

First graders learn with their hands and their senses. Each week has one question, one outdoor or table job, and three trios (3 + 3 + 3). They need a fast win, so we plant radishes in September and harvest them in December. Cool-season food (lettuce, peas, garlic, carrots) goes in during fall. Warm-season joy (beans, sunflowers, cherry tomatoes) starts in trays in January and goes outside in March. We end by saving seed and leaving the bed kind for next year’s class.

This plan is written for Room 17’s garden, Cupertino-area weather, and CUSD Tuesday dates. It does not follow an old class log. Shift a planting week if the bed is already full or the soil is soaked.

If the week goes sideways

  • Only one grownup: skip trios. Whole group of 9 does the table job, then 6 minutes at the bed with trowels put away.
  • Rain: do the journal / smell / seed-split / jar / color-climber version first. Plant the next dry Tuesday.
  • Nothing came up: still count, still draw “bare soil,” plant again. The science is the noticing.
  • Group B starts late: drop the look step. Keep the do step.
